How the radar works

A transparent, fully-funded filter — not a scholarship aggregator.

  • ● Sources are verified official pages (governments, universities, scholarship hubs) plus curated PhD databases such as FindAPhD, EURAXESS, and Nature Careers.
  • ● Every page must show full funding — tuition plus a stipend or living allowance. Tuition-only awards are filtered out.
  • ● The result must be open to international applicants (not home-country only), with a clear eligibility statement.
  • ● Deadlines are inferred per programme cycle, with open scholarships surfaced first and closed ones pushed to the bottom.
